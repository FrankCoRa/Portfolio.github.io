## Selected Research & Analytical Projects

### 1. Educational Enrollment Forecasting
**Machine Learning | XGBoost | Python | Educational Analytics**

Developed season-specific enrollment forecasting models using more than 17,000 historical course-section records.

The models achieved held-out term validation R² values of approximately 0.74–0.83, demonstrating the potential of historical institutional data to support academic planning.

Beyond predictive performance, the project motivates research questions about temporal validation, enrollment uncertainty, institutional constraints, and the relationship between observed enrollment and actual student demand.

### 2. Institutional Analytics & Resource Optimization
**Python | SQL | Tableau | Academic Scheduling**

Developed analytical workflows supporting classroom recommendations, course capacity analysis, and institutional scheduling.

These projects integrate historical patterns, enrollment estimates, room availability, and operational constraints to support academic decision-making.

### 3. Housing Expenditure & Socioeconomic Analysis
**R | Statistical Analysis | PCA | Regression**

Investigated relationships between household characteristics and housing expenditures using quantitative analysis and interactive visualizations.

The project reflects my interest in applying statistical methods to socioeconomic questions and communicating complex findings through accessible analytical tools.

### 4. Humanitarian Data Analysis — Somalia
**R | Exploratory Analysis | Data Visualization**

Contributed to a collaborative analytical project examining humanitarian conditions affecting internally displaced populations in Somalia.

The project demonstrates the application of quantitative methods to interdisciplinary social challenges.

### 5. Healthcare Feasibility & Reverse Logistics Research
**Industrial Engineering | Economic Analysis | Sustainability**

My undergraduate research investigated the economic and environmental feasibility of medical oxygen self-sufficiency and reverse logistics in healthcare organizations in Arequipa, Peru.

This work introduced me to formal research design, resource allocation, and evidence-based decision-making.

## Research Philosophy

I believe that useful research requires more than accurate models or sophisticated technology.

It requires understanding the questions being asked, evaluating the assumptions behind analytical methods, acknowledging uncertainty, and communicating findings responsibly.

My goal is to contribute to research that makes statistical and computational methods more reliable, interpretable, and useful for educational and social decision-making.
