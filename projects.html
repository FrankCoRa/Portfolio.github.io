Education · Predictive modeling

Enrollment Forecasting for Academic Scheduling

Research or operational question
How accurately can historical course-section records predict future enrollment, and what are the limits of those predictions?
Approach
Season-specific XGBoost models using historical academic scheduling and enrollment records; time-aware feature engineering and validation.
Evidence and contribution
More than 17,000 historical course-section records; reported term-level validation R² approximately 0.739–0.826. These are predictive metrics, not causal effects.
Limitations and next steps
Course capacity, scheduling changes, missing records, and potential leakage require careful assessment. A future research extension would compare simpler baselines, quantify uncertainty, and investigate whether course availability changes student outcomes.

EDUCATIONAL ANALYTICS · INSTITUTIONAL RESEARCH

Academic Enrollment Risk & Capacity Utilization Analysis

Research or operational question
How can enrollment-to-capacity patterns identify underutilized academic sections and inform institutional scheduling and resource allocation?
Approach
Applied enrollment risk classification and exploratory analysis using SQL, Banner SIS, Argos, and interactive Tableau dashboards.
Evidence and contribution
Developed three visualizations to monitor enrollment risk, capacity utilization, and school-level patterns using 25% and 50% thresholds.
Limitations and next steps
Enrollment patterns are descriptive rather than causal. Future research could integrate longitudinal analysis, machine learning enrollment forecasts, and classroom allocation optimization to strengthen predictive decision-making.

Social science · Statistical modeling

Expenditures and Socioeconomic Patterns

Research or operational question
How are household characteristics associated with housing expenditures and affordability?
Approach
Exploratory analysis, correlation, principal component analysis, regression, and R Shiny visualizations using U.S. Consumer Expenditure Survey data.
Evidence and contribution
The original portfolio describes 2022–2023 survey data, more than 40,000 records, and over 800 variables.
Limitations and next steps
Observational associations should not be interpreted as causal; survey design, sampling weights, variable definitions, and model diagnostics merit further documentation.
