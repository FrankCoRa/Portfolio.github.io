Research direction

Research Interests

I want to investigate when quantitative evidence is reliable enough to guide institutional decisions—and how to improve the methods used to produce that evidence.

Statistical learning & model evaluation

Prediction across academic terms, temporal validation, uncertainty, calibration, data leakage prevention, and performance under changing institutional conditions.

Longitudinal & causal methods

Understanding educational progression and the difference between associations in observational records and the effects of institutional interventions.

Measurement & educational evaluation

Examining how institutional indicators are defined, how missing data affect conclusions, and how evaluation design can support credible evidence.

Computational social science & information systems

Using computational tools to study human and institutional systems while considering data quality, interpretation, governance, and responsible deployment.

Potential doctoral research questions

How does course availability affect observed enrollment and academic progression? How do forecasting models perform when scheduling practices change? How can institutions evaluate resource-allocation interventions using observational data? These are future research directions, not claims of completed causal studies.
